Privacy Policy for Veloryn Launcher\n Effective Date: May 23, 2026\n\n 1. Introduction\n Veloryn Launcher is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.\n\n 2. Data Collection and Usage\n Veloryn Launcher follows a \"Privacy-by-Design\" approach:\n - Local Processing: All data analysis, including \"Fraud Guard\" and \"Suggested Apps\", is performed locally on your device.\n - No Cloud Sync: We do not use external servers to store your app lists, usage habits, or launcher settings.\n - Personal Data: We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII).\n\n 3. Core Features\n - App Lock: Your security credentials (PIN/Password) are stored locally using Android\’s encrypted preferences.\n - Fraud Guard: Analyzes newly installed apps for risks on-device only.\n - System Widgets: Accesses battery and storage status in real-time without storing history.\n\n 4. Permissions\n - Query All Packages: Necessary to show your apps in the library.\n - Battery/Storage: Required for the home screen system widgets.\n - No Internet Required: The app functions entirely offline for maximum security.\n\n 5. Third-Party Services\n This app does not contain third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or trackers.\n\n 6. Contact\n For questions, reach out to us at morasonailabs@gmail.com
